Questions

Good to know

What's your service area?

Smithfield, Selma, Clayton, Four Oaks and the surrounding Johnston County area. Message us if you're just outside. We can often still help.

Do you take dogs of any size?

Yes. We've cared for everything from small breeds to large working dogs. Just let us know what to expect.

What about medications?

Happy to give oral medication at no extra charge. For injections, let us know in advance so we can talk it through.

Can I meet you first?

Please do. We do a free meet & greet at your home before the first booking, so your dog gets to know us with you there.

What should I leave out for you?

Their usual food, any medication with written instructions, their leash, and your vet's details. We'll go over the rest at the meet & greet.

How far ahead should I book?

The sooner the better for holidays and school breaks, which fill up first. For ordinary weeks a few days' notice is usually plenty, but just ask, since we can often fit in last-minute stays.
