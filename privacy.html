Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

Last updated: September 9, 2026

The short version

We collect the contact and booking details you give us — through our website contact form, Facebook/Instagram lead ads, text, or phone — only to respond to your inquiry and arrange your dog's care. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone outside of running this business.

What we collect

Depending on how you reach out, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Details about your dog(s) and the care you're requesting (dates, services, temperament notes, vet info)
  • Your address, if needed for house sitting, drop-ins, or walks
  • Payment information, handled through our payment processor — we do not store card numbers ourselves

How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Respond to booking requests and answer questions
  • Schedule and provide the pet care services you request
  • Send appointment confirmations, reminders, or updates about your dog while in our care
  • Keep basic records for our own business and tax purposes

We do not use your information for anything beyond running Dillon's Pet Care — no third-party advertising lists, no data resale.

Facebook & Instagram lead forms

If you submit a lead form through one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, Meta shares the information you enter (name, phone, email, and any answers you give) directly with us so we can follow up. That submission is also subject to Meta's own privacy policy, which covers how they handle data on their platforms.

Who sees your information

Only Hunter and Dillon, the two people who run this business. We may use standard third-party tools to help operate the business — such as a form or scheduling service — but we don't authorize them to use your information for anything other than helping us serve you.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your contact information at any time, opt out of any future texts or emails, or ask what information we have on file for you. Just reach out using the contact info below.
